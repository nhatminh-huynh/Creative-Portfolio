    ↘ From August 2025, I had the opportunity to collaborate with Tung Monkey and his team, representing Vietnam at iMapp Bucharest in Romania, the world's largest projection mapping event, through the artwork 'Chợ – When Art Is Life'. Our concept centered on Vietnamese culture, and the scenes I worked on focused on Tò He and Hát Bội masks.

    Later, my teammate Ton Ng and I participated in several projection mapping projects. Our artwork 'ZEN' explored the concept of inner breathing—where human breath and the rhythm of nature merge into a shared soul. The project was showcased at SCG Home Experience as part of Bangkok Design Week 2026.

    Following that, our artwork 'N[e]ostalgia Tableau' was selected for OFFF Barcelona 2026 and screened at Disseny Hub in Spain. The work presents a retro-futuristic domestic tableau in which familiar household objects are reimagined within an imagined future.
