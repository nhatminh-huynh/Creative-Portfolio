    ↘ 'Mannequin' was a photography project I did during high school as the Art Director of the Photography Club—'Lumiere'.

    Inspired by mannequins as a form of visual art, the project featured a disco-ball-skinned mannequin as its central character, symbolizing reflection, creativity, and transformation. Through this concept, we explored Lumiere's identity from two perspectives: art and innovation. Just as mannequins embody the spirit of an era through fashion, Lumiere represents passion, talent, and a commitment to embracing new creative challenges through continuous evolution.
