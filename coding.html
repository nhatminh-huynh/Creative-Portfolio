    ↘ For me, art is not only about the creator—it is also about inviting the audience to become part of the creative process. This belief led me to explore interactive art, a field I have grown deeply passionate about.

    I primarily work with TouchDesigner and VS Code (using p5.js) to develop interactive and self-generative pieces that respond to audience input. By incorporating elements of randomness and unpredictability, my works continuously evolve, creating a different experience each time they are viewed or performed.

    Through this balance between intention and chance, I create spaces where audiences can actively engage with the work, becoming contributors to the creative experience rather than passive observers.
