    ↘ 'Nước Mộng' is a client solution project focused on developing an MVP to address the declining public interest in traditional art forms, especially among younger generations, which poses a risk to cultural continuity.

    While rich in historical and cultural value, these art forms often lack interactivity and accessibility. To better understand audience needs, we conducted user surveys and used the insights to create an interactive water puppetry experience enhanced by hand-tracking technology.

    The result is an immersive experience designed to re-engage modern audiences and foster deeper appreciation for Vietnamese traditional arts through active participation.
