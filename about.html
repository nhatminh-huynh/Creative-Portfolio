    Nhat Minh
    /visual artist/

    ★ Huynh Truc Nhat Minh is a multimedia visual artist based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Their practice draws strong inspiration from net.art—a digital art form that emerged in the 1990s using the internet as both medium and subject—and the emotional presence of computers as cultural artifacts. Nhat Minh is also deeply interested in conceptual art, especially works where the audience takes an active role in the creative process rather than remaining passive observers.

    ★ Their work combines playful experimentation with cultural reflection, often exploring the Vina-Manifesto—an ongoing approach that incorporates Vietnamese culture and everyday life to inform and expand their artistic concepts.

    ★ Currently a second-year student at the Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology, Nhat Minh continues to experiment across multiple platforms and mediums, embracing the fluid boundaries between digital and physical forms.
