    ↘ This was one of the most incredible experiences I’ve ever had. I learned how to combine music, beats, and visuals in a cohesive way. We used Resolume for VJing, while the visuals were created using Blender, After Effects, and Photoshop. The concept poses a satirical question: What would be left of the world if mothers stopped warning their kids about dirty street food, if all 100 million people in Vietnam owned a red or blue plastic chair, and if limbs were boxed up and sold at a discount because no one needed them anymore? You're invited to count down the days… to the end of the world.
