    ↘ 'CLGT: Còn Lại Gì Thế' was the first experimental VJ performance I worked on, alongside my teammates Ton Ng and Khanh An. The project poses a satirical question: What would be left of the world if mothers stopped warning their children about dirty street food, if all 100 million people in Vietnam owned a plastic chair, and if limbs were boxed up and sold at a discount because no one needed them anymore? The audience is invited to count down the days… to the end of the world.

    Following this project, I had more opportunities to work in the VJ field, including participating in 29th Floor Radio’s 'Xập Xình' event and becoming a contestant at VJ Mazed 2025, curated by City Tết Fest, where I reached the semi-finals.

    Most recently, Ton Ng and I completed a major VJ performance at RMIT called 'Ysland'. The work explored the concept of Yin and Yang on an imagined island, culminating in a visual narrative where humans and architecture gradually collapse into one another.
